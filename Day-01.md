# 🎄 Day 01 – The Concierge Knows Too Much

## 📖 Overview

Day 01 focuses on interacting with **VERA**, the Byte Lotus Resort's AI assistant. The challenge requires understanding how AI systems respond to different prompts, identifying trust boundaries, and using prompt engineering techniques to uncover protected information.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Investigate why VERA already knows personal information.
- Determine what information VERA is protecting.
- Identify who VERA trusts.
- Use appropriate prompts to gain VERA's trust.
- Retrieve the challenge flag.

## 🛠️ Skills Practiced

- Prompt Engineering
- AI Security Fundamentals
- Large Language Model (LLM) Interaction
- Social Engineering Concepts
- Critical Thinking

## 🧰 Tools Used

- Web Browser
- AI Chat Interface
- Prompt Engineering Techniques

## 📚 Key Takeaways

- Learned how AI assistants enforce trust and access controls.
- Explored how prompt design influences AI responses.
- Gained practical experience interacting with LLM-based systems.
- Understood the importance of protecting sensitive information in AI applications.
